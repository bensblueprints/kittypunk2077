- Calm, brilliant, and dangerously curious

## Background

Dr. Muzzle is the brilliant yet morally bankrupt head of research at Goodboy Global. He leads the canine augmentation and implant program, developing behavior-control technology that can modify, enhance, or even override free will. His custom muzzle is a top-secret device with capabilities far beyond what the public knows… and its true purpose will be revealed in a later season.
