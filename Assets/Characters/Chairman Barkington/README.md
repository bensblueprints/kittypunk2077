## Background

The ruthless and charismatic CEO of Goodboy Global, the world's largest canine megacorporation. A master strategist and corporate manipulator, he believes order, profit, and canine superiority are the natural state of the world. To him, cats are a disruptive variable that must be contained… or eliminated.
