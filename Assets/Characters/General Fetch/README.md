# General Fetch

**Faction:** Goodboy Global (antagonist)
**Role:** Commander of Goodboy Global's global security forces
**Personality & traits:**
- Militaristic and highly disciplined
- Ruthless enforcer, zero tolerance
- Loyal to Chairman Barkington
- Strategist with battlefield experience
- Views cats as a dangerous virus
- Proud, intense, and fearless
- Believes strength maintains order

## Background

General Fetch is the iron fist of Goodboy Global. A decorated former special forces operative, Fetch now commands the corporation's global security forces. He is disciplined, ruthless, and obsessed with eliminating the cat resistance. Where Chairman Barkington controls the strategy, Fetch makes sure it happens — by any means necessary.
